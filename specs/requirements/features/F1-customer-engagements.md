# Customer engagements

## Purpose

A requester raises an engagement request from a CRM opportunity, edits it, and tracks, completes or cancels it.

## User Stories

- F1.1 As a requester, I raise a customer engagement request from a CRM opportunity.
- F1.2 As a requester, I choose the engagement's type from the five customer engagement types.
- F1.3 As a requester, I edit my engagement request while it is open.
- F1.4 As a requester, I see my engagement requests and where each one stands.
- F1.5 As a requester, I complete an engagement when its work is done.
- F1.6 As a requester, I cancel an engagement I no longer need.

## Decisions

- There are five customer engagement types, not three.
- A customer engagement needs no approval; allocation can start as soon as it is raised.
- The CRM is reached as a service the business already holds; which CRM is not yet decided.

## Open Questions

2. Does cancelling an engagement require a reason, and what happens to its allocations?
